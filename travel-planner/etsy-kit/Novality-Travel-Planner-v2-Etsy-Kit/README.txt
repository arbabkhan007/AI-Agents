# ETSY LISTING KIT — Travel Planner v2 (Novality store)

This kit has everything you need to publish the listing today:

```
├── README.txt                  ← you are here (start here)
├── listing-copy.md             ← ready-to-paste title, description & 13 tags
├── 1-listing-images/           ← 10 square listing images (2000×2000) + Pinterest bonus
├── 2-shop-branding/            ← shop avatar / logo (1000×1000)
├── 3-product-files/            ← THE DIGITAL FILES YOU UPLOAD TO ETSY (.xlsx + buyer guide)
└── 4-source-code/              ← full Python source that generates the workbook & images
```

## Publish in 6 steps

1. Etsy → Shop Manager → Listings → Add a listing.
2. Photos: upload `1-listing-images/01-cover.png` FIRST (it becomes the
   thumbnail), then 02–10 in order. Add `pinterest-1000x1500.png` when you
   pin the listing later.
3. Copy the title, description and 13 tags from `listing-copy.md`.
4. Set type: DIGITAL DOWNLOAD. Upload the two files from `3-product-files/`
   (the .xlsx and START-HERE-How-To-Use.txt). Etsy delivers them instantly
   after purchase.
5. Price: digital planners like this commonly sell at $6–$15. Launch around
   $8.99 with a 30% launch sale, then settle at $11.99.
6. Category: Paper & Party Supplies > Paper > Calendars & Planners
   (or Books, Movies & Music > Books > Instruction Books & Guides).

## Good to know

- The workbook's formula cells are locked; the unlock password is
  `premium`. Keep it private — buyers never need it (input cells are
  free to edit, protection just stops accidental breakage).
- Personal-use license is stated in the listing copy. For a commercial
  license option, duplicate the listing at a higher price.
- Regenerate or customize everything: see `4-source-code/ALL_SOURCE_CODE.txt`
  (pure Python, no paid tools needed — `python3 build_travel_planner.py`
  rebuilds the spreadsheet, `make_listing_images.py` redraws all images).
