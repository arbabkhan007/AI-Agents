Thank you for your purchase — happy travels! Here's how to get the
most out of your planner in 5 minutes.

──────────────────────────────
1. OPEN THE FILE
──────────────────────────────
• Double-click "Travel Planner v2.xlsx" — it opens in Microsoft Excel
  (2016+, 365, Mac or web).
• Start on the HOME sheet: it links to all 28 planning sheets.

──────────────────────────────
2. THE TWO GOLDEN RULES
──────────────────────────────
• YELLOW cells are yours — type, pick dates, choose from dropdowns.
• BLUE-GRAY cells calculate automatically — they're protected so the
  formulas can never be broken. Nothing to fix, ever.

──────────────────────────────
3. RECOMMENDED ORDER
──────────────────────────────
① Destination Overview  → who, where, when (feeds the Home dashboard)
② Budget Tracker        → set estimated costs per category
③ Savings Goal          → log deposits, watch the bar fill
④ Book flights & stays  → log them in Booking Log / Transportation /
                          Accommodation (Wi-Fi passwords live here too!)
⑤ Daily Itinerary       → 80 slots; transport & booking dropdowns
⑥ Packing Checklist     → 59 items pre-loaded, tick as you pack
⑦ On the trip           → log spend daily in the Expense Log (any
                          currency — it converts automatically!)
⑧ Home again            → Travel Journal, Ratings & Souvenir lists

──────────────────────────────
4. QUICK TIPS
──────────────────────────────
• Dropdown cells show a small arrow when selected — tap ✓ when done.
• On the Currency Converter sheet, update the rates before you travel;
  the Expense Log reads that table automatically.
• Want a fresh trip? Save a copy of the file (File → Save As) and
  clear the yellow cells — use it for every future adventure.
• Print any sheet: pages are already print-friendly.
