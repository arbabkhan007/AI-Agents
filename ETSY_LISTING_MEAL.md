## 2. Description

```
Stop deciding what to cook at six o'clock.

This is a 36 tab Excel workbook that plans your meals for the week, works
out what they cost, writes your shopping list and keeps an eye on what is
in the fridge. Plan breakfast, lunch and dinner for the week and everything
else follows: the calories and protein for the week, the cost of every
meal, a grocery list already grouped by aisle with the quantities scaled to
your household, and a pantry that tells you what to use before it goes off.

WHAT IT DOES

- Weekly planner and monthly planner - pick the meals for the week, day by
  day, or lay out the whole month with a theme and a budget for each week.
- Recipe book with costings - one row per recipe with the timings, the
  nutrition per serving and the cost per serving, calculated from the
  ingredients and their prices. Change a price once and every recipe that
  uses it re-prices itself.
- Automatic grocery list - everything you planned is already there, reduced
  by what you already have, grouped by aisle, with pack counts and a
  running total. Print it and tick it off at the shop.
- Pantry with use-by dates - add what you buy and the shelf life works out
  the expiry date, then flags anything inside the "use it or lose it"
  window.
- Food budget - planned versus actual spend, week by week and month by
  month, against the weekly budget you set.
- Nutrition log - daily calories, protein, carbs, fat, fibre, sugar and
  sodium against the targets you set for your household.
- Leftovers and batch cooking - what is in the fridge or freezer, when to
  eat it, and a prep day to-do list in the order to do it.
- Food waste tracker - what you binned, why, and what it cost, so you can
  see where the money is going.
- Store price comparison - the same item priced at every shop, converted
  to a price per unit.
- Dietary filters - allergies and dietary needs, checked automatically
  against every recipe and every planned meal.
- Printable pages - a weekly meal plan for the fridge door, a grocery list
  with tick boxes, and a recipe card.

HOW IT ARRIVES

An instant download: a .xlsx file you open in Excel, plus a blank copy with
no example data if you would rather start from scratch. Every formula is
already written - about 79,000 of them - and every cell you need to type in
is a different colour from the ones that calculate themselves.

Set it up once: your household, your budget, your nutrition targets, your
ingredients and prices, your recipes. After that it is five minutes a week.

Works in Excel 2010 and later, Google Sheets, LibreOffice and Apple
Numbers. No macros, no add-ins, nothing to install.

Please note: this is a digital download. No physical product will be
shipped. Because it is a spreadsheet it cannot be refunded, so do ask if
you have any questions before you buy.
```
