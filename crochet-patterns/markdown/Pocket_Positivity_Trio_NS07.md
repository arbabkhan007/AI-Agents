# Pocket Positivity Trio

> **3 mini patterns Easy / beginner 20 - 35 min each**

Three palm-sized amigurumi that slip into a coat pocket - Sunny the Sunflower, Waddle the Penguin and Spud the Potato. They share one easy construction and work up in an evening or two.

**THE SET**  Sunny ~3.0 cm across the petals  ·  Waddle ~3.8 cm tall  ·  Spud ~2.7 cm long. #4 worsted on a 3.5 mm hook. Sunny and Waddle need no sewn pieces; Spud is worked flat and closed in one seam.

---

SAFETY - SMALL PARTS
The set uses 5 mm black safety eyes (six in total) - small parts and a choking hazard, not intended for
children under 3. For young children, embroider the eyes and mouths instead. Sellers: do not market as
"baby-safe"; sell as adult collectibles, desk companions or keychain charms, and tag with materials,
your maker name and "Not suitable for children under 3 years."

### Materials & gauge

- **Yarn** #4 worsted (~250 m / 100 g). The three toys use roughly 8 m total (under 4 g), so scraps are fine.
Golden yellow + chocolate (Sunny); black + cream chest + yellow beak (Waddle); warm tan (Spud).
- **Hook & eyes** 3.5 mm (US E/4). Six 5 mm black safety eyes, or embroidery thread.
- **Also** about 3 g fibre fill total, blunt tapestry needle, stitch marker, scissors.
- **Gauge** about 3.5 mm per stitch and 3.2 mm per round in sc. Pocket size means gauge shows - work a
swatch; the stated sizes follow from it.

### Techniques

Continuous spiral rounds with a marker (no joins). Magic-ring start for Sunny's center and Waddle's body; invisible
decreases; petal CLUSTERS worked into one stitch with a slip stitch between petals; an OVAL worked around a
chain for Spud; and simple sewing/embroidery. Abbreviations: MR magic ring · sc · hdc · inc (2 sc in one st) · dec
(invisible decrease) · sl st · ch · sts.

A small brown cushion; nine yellow petals are worked straight into Rnd 3.

### Petals - yellow (worked in the round)

| Rnd | Instruction | Sts | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| Petal | join yellow with a sl st into any st of Rnd 3, then [sl st in next st, (sc, hdc, sc) in next st] x 9 | (36) | 9 petals |

Each repeat uses 2 stitches (a slip stitch, then three stitches into the next), so nine petals close the 18-stitch round
exactly. The slip stitch sits in the valley between petals; work this round loosely (use a 4 mm hook for this round
only if it pulls tight). FO and weave in. Face: embroider a smiling mouth between Rnd 2-3 and add eyes 3 stitches
apart on Rnd 2 (~10.5 mm; use 4 apart only if embroidering).

### Wings, chest patch & beak

Wings (black, make 2): 4 sc in MR (4); [sc, inc] x 2 (6); sc around for 2 rnd (6). FO with a 15 cm tail, do not stuff; sew
to the sides at Rnd 6 angled backward.
Chest patch (cream, flat oval): ch 6, sc in 2nd ch, 3 sc, 3 sc in last ch, then down the other side 3 sc, 2 sc in last ch
(12); Rnd 2: inc, 3 sc, inc x3, 3 sc, inc x2 (18). FO with a tail; sew onto the front covering Rnds 4-8, stitching top and
bottom edges only so the middle puffs.
Beak (yellow): ch 2, 3 sc in 2nd ch (3); inc x3 (6); sc around (6). FO, do not stuff; sew centred between the eyes,
angled slightly down. Eyes 3 stitches apart on Rnd 3; add a touch of pink blush sparingly.

| Rnd | Instruction | Sts | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| Found. | ch 7 | chain |  |
| R1 | sc in 2nd ch, 4 sc, 3 sc in last ch; other side: 4 sc, 2 sc in last ch | (14) | around the chain |
| R2 | inc, 4 sc, inc x3, 4 sc, inc x2 | (20) |  |
| R3-6 | sc in each st around (4 rnd) | (20) |  |
| R7 | [3 sc, dec] x 4 | (16) |  |
| R8 | [2 sc, dec] x 4 | (12) |  |
| R9 | [sc, dec] x 4 | (8) | begin stuffing |
| R10 | dec x 4 | (4) | close the seam |

Closing: leave a 20 cm tail. With the body flat, whip-stitch the remaining opening closed along the flat top so the
seam reads as the potato's natural crease; weave the end inside. Face: eyes about 5 stitches apart on the broad
side around Rnd 4 (Spud's face is ~27 mm wide, so 4 or 5 apart both fit), with a small open mouth below.
Making a bigger trio: use chunky/aran or hold #4 double on a 3.5 mm hook (~4.5 mm/stitch, about 1.3x): Sunny ~3.9
cm, Waddle ~4.9 cm, Spud ~3.5 cm. Stitch counts do not change.
Selling & care
Sell as adult collectibles, desk companions or keychain charms - not "baby-safe". Stuff firmly and evenly. Spot
clean with a damp cloth; do not machine wash; fluff pulled loops with a clean slicker brush or toothbrush.
Spud is worked as an oval around a chain, then flat-stitched closed so the seam reads as a natural crease.

- **Petal edges curl / will not close.** Use invisible decreases on the center's Rnd 5 and pull the closing snug. Nine
petal repeats use exactly 18 stitches (two each) - check Rnd 3 is (18) and
you slip-stitched once per repeat.
- **Petals sit flat.** Work the three stitches in one stitch loosely, or use a 4 mm hook for the petal round only.
- **Toys come out smaller / larger.** Tighter than 3.5 mm/stitch: go up half a hook size or hold yarn double.
Looser: drop to a 3 mm hook.
- **Waddle's wings flop.** Sew at Rnd 6 and catch a stitch of the body with each pass.
- **Spud's seam shows.** Whip-stitch along the flat top so the seam reads as a crease, and stuff evenly before
closing.
- **Eyes too near the edge.** On Sunny and Waddle place them three stitches apart, not four.
Before you finish
Check each toy: Sunny has 9 even petals and a closed brown center; Waddle has 2 wings, a cream chest
and a yellow beak; Spud's seam reads as a crease. All eyes are locked or embroidered and every end is
woven in at least 5 cm.
