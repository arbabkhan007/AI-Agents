DAYCARE & CHILDCARE BUSINESS MANAGEMENT SPREADSHEET
Novality Store

FILES
- Daycare-Demo.xlsx: example records, dated October 2026. Use it to explore.
- Daycare-Blank.xlsx: empty registers; use this as your starting template.

START HERE
1. Open the blank workbook in Microsoft Excel and save your own copy. Allow
   formulas to recalculate when it opens. The dashboard and reports update
   from the registers after recalculation.
2. Read the Getting Started tab. Set the centre name, reporting year, local
   age bands and reference ratios in Settings & Lists. Default ratios are
   examples, not recommendations or licensing standards.
3. Use a unique Family ID, Child ID and Staff ID consistently. Enter data in
   white cells; shaded cells calculate automatically. Registers have filters
   and fixed capacity. The headers are on row 6.
4. Add each invoice, payment, credit, write-off, deposit or refund as one
   ledger row with a positive amount, a unique transaction ID, date and family
   ID. Resolve the Review column warnings. Amounts include any applicable tax.
   A deposit is already a credit: do not record it again as a payment when
   applied. Refunds increase the family balance and reduce cash. Reconcile
   with your bank and accounting system.
5. Monthly and annual reports summarize the date of each transaction. Net
   charges = invoices minus credits and write-offs. Net cash = payments plus
   deposits minus refunds. Cash less costs subtracts expenses and payroll.
   Do not also enter payroll in expenses or costs will be counted twice.

IMPORTANT LIMITATIONS
- Settings for tax, fees, age bands and payroll burden are references; they do
  not automatically create invoices, calculate tax, apply discounts, or calculate
  employer payroll costs. Enter amounts manually and verify them.
- Staff Schedule and Classroom Management are planning tools only. They do
  not measure actual live coverage, calculate compliance, or guarantee safety.
- Pickup names are notes, not proof of authorization. Verify identity and
  authorization independently before releasing a child.
- Sensitive records need proper encrypted, access-controlled storage and
  backups. Worksheet protection is not encryption or access control. Formula
  protection password: premium. Inputs are unlocked, formulas locked.
- This workbook does not send messages, process payments, file returns, or
  replace official medical, safeguarding, payroll or accounting records. It is
  not medical, legal, licensing, tax, payroll or accounting advice.
- The workbook is built with Excel 2010-compatible functions. It was checked
  structurally and its key money formulas were tested, but it was not opened
  in Windows Excel during generation. Please confirm it opens normally in
  your version before entering real information.

CAPACITY (rows per register)
Children 150; families 200; waitlist 120; attendance 2,000; tuition 700;
ledger 700; daily reports 600; meals 600; medical 180; incidents 150;
staff 60; schedule 750; payroll 350; rooms 30; expenses 500; inventory 180;
communication 350; documents 500. Do not enter beyond the prepared rows:
reports and formulas only include those ranges. IDs are not generated and
unique IDs are not enforced except for ledger review warnings.
