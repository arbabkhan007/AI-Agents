## 2. Listing description

Paste this into the description box. The first 160 characters are what shows
in search results, so the opening sentence does the selling.

```text
Run your whole pet sitting and dog walking business from one spreadsheet.
Twenty-seven tabs that price every job, book it, log the visit, invoice the
client and count the money - so you spend your time with the animals instead
of in admin.

WHY THIS ONE
Most templates track one thing. This one runs the business end to end:
book a job and the price calculates itself from your own service list, the
invoice writes itself, the payment status updates itself, and the dashboard,
reports and budget all move on their own. No formulas to write, ever.

THE 27 TABS
Start Here - what everything does, in one screen
Dashboard - revenue, expenses, profit, upcoming jobs, overdue invoices
Settings - your prices, tax rate, mileage rate and working hours
Services - your own price list; change a price once, it changes everywhere
Clients and Pets - contacts, keys, feeding, medication, allergies, vet details
Bookings - the schedule, with a clash check that flags double bookings
Visit Log - check in and out times, hours, distance, outcome, client rating
Health Log - weight, medication, vaccinations and treatments per pet
Incidents and Messages - what went wrong, and what you told the client
Invoices and Payments - invoices, balances, ageing, part payments, receipts
Expenses and Mileage - what you spent and what you can claim back
Staff - sitters, hours worked, commission and pay due
Inventory - stock levels and reorder alerts
Reminders - bookings due, invoices overdue, stock low, certificates expiring
Reports - profit and loss, revenue by service, client ageing, sitter
performance, for any date range you pick
Budget - targets, budget versus actual, forecast and cash flow
Print Invoice, Print Pet Care, Print Visit, Print Forms - five ready to
print documents
Help - every column explained

HOW IT WORKS
Type into the cream coloured cells only. Everything grey is calculated and
locked. Each sheet is protected with the password "premium" so the maths
cannot be typed over by accident - unprotect it any time from the Review tab.

TWO FILES INCLUDED
1. Filled with example data - nine clients, twelve pets, thirty-two
   bookings and twenty-three invoices, so every chart and report shows real
   numbers the moment you open it. Delete the example rows when you are
   ready, or keep them as a guide.
2. Completely blank - the same workbook with empty registers, ready for your
   own data from day one.

YOURS, NOT OURS
Set your business name, currency, distance unit, tax rate, mileage rate and
password. Choose kilometres or miles. Add as many rows as you need.

REQUIREMENTS
Microsoft Excel 2010 or newer, LibreOffice Calc, Google Sheets or Apple
Numbers. No add-ins, no macros, no subscription. Formulas are kept simple on
purpose - no dynamic arrays - so it opens everywhere.

INSTANT DOWNLOAD
Nothing is posted to you. You get the files the moment payment clears.

Please note: this is a digital product. No physical item will be shipped.
Due to the nature of digital downloads, refunds cannot be offered, but if
anything does not work for you message me and I will fix it.
```
